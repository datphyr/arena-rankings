About

What is this?

Arena Rankings is an automated ratings service for the arena FPS scene. It discovers and downloads match/tournament pages from plusforward.net, parses the results into structured data, and computes Elo and Glicko-2 ratings that are served through this web site, a JSON API, and Discord/Twitch chat bots. The pipeline is game-agnostic and tracks multiple arena shooters side by side — each with its own leaderboards and ratings.

How ratings are calculated

Elo

Standard Elo with a variable K-factor: how much your rating moves per match depends on your experience and on the tier of the tournament you played.

Final K = base K(games) × tier multiplier. Expected score follows the standard logistic curve E = 1 / (1 + 10(R_opp − R)/400), and each match updates R′ = R + K·(score − E). Everyone starts at 1500. These numbers (the K-factors and tier multipliers above) are all tunable — the exact values shown are what this site runs today.

Worked example

Say Alice (1700 Elo, 150 games played) beats Bob (1500 Elo, 40 games) in a premier tournament:

  • Alice: K = 16 × 2.0 = 32  ·  E = 1/(1+10^(−200/400)) ≈ 0.76  →  +32 × (1 − 0.76) ≈ +7.7
  • Bob:   K = 24 × 2.0 = 48  ·  E = 1/(1+10^(+200/400)) ≈ 0.24  →  −48 × (0.24 − 0) ≈ −11.5
  • Alice → 1707.7  ·  Bob → 1488.5

Glicko-2

Glicko-2 is a more careful system. Instead of a single number, every player has three: a rating, a rating deviation (RD) — how sure we are — and a volatility — how consistent they are. It updates once per rating period — currently monthly — using all the games in that period at once (based on the Glicko-2 paper). A system constant, τ = 1.2, controls how much a player's volatility is allowed to swing between periods (the range is 0.2 = very stable to 1.2 = very volatile).

What the RD really means:

The lower the RD, the more confident we are in a rating. The leaderboard shows the cautious lower end (rating − RD), so an uncertain player can't sit artificially high:

Confident — 1800 ± 25tight range → stable
Fresh — 1500 ± 350huge range → unknown

Every game played shrinks the range. Sitting out a month grows it again (back toward 350). Only players with ≥ 30 matches appear on the Glicko-2 leaderboard, so you never see a rating we don't really trust yet.

Worked example

Alice (1700 ± 40) plays 5 matches this month: wins 3, loses 2, against a field averaging ~1550 with their own RDs. Because Glicko-2 weighs each opponent by their uncertainty, beating a high-RD player moves your rating less than beating a proven one.

Result: rating 1700 → ~1731,  RD 40 → ~28  (more games = more confidence)

The RD drop matters as much as the rating gain — after more matches, 1731 is a trusted number, not a hopeful one.

Features
  • Leaderboards per game, sortable and filterable, with an as-of date filter that rebuilds the standings as they were on any past day.
  • All-time peaks — every player's single highest Elo and Glicko-2 rating, tracked across the site.
  • Player pages — rating-history charts, map edges (per-map win-rate vs overall, green = overperform / red = underperform), recent matches and VOD links.
  • Head-to-head — direct records between two players, including net rating gained or lost across their meetings and per-map results.
  • Rivals — a player's most frequent and toughest opponents at a glance.
  • Rating deltas — see how much a specific match or tournament moved each player's rating.
  • A JSON API (Swagger at /api/docs).
  • Discord slash commands and Twitch chat commands for ratings on the go.
